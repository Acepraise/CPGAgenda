Coalition for Participatory Governance (CPG)

Town Hall
Meeting Agenda

Stakeholder Engagement on Local Government Development Partnership

📅 7th May, 2026 ⏰ 11:00 AM 📍 Lafiaji Community Hall, Obalende, Lagos Island

Participatory Governance:
A Critical Tool for Inclusivity,
Peace & Development

Master of Ceremony: Dr Alexander Akhigbe
Founder/CEO – African Cleanup Initiative & Chair, Nigeria CSO Strategy Group on SDGs (Office of the SSA to the President on SDGs)


This Town Hall brings together civil society, government officials, legislators, traditional leaders, youth organisations, private sector actors and development partners to strengthen participatory governance at the grassroots level.

Host: Chairman, Lagos Island Local Government


Chairman of the Occasion: Pastor Ituah Ighodalo
President, African Leadership Group (ALG)


The event centres on the analysis of the Lagos Island LGA 2026 Budget, showcasing citizen-focused budget monitoring tools developed by BudgIT.
